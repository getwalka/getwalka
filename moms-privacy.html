Privacy Policy

Walka, Inc. (“Walka”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) · Effective Date: November 3rd, 2026 · Applies to getwalka.com/moms only. For the Walka mobile app's own Privacy Policy (HealthKit step data, leaderboards, payouts), see the main Walka Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Walka collects, uses, and shares information when you visit getwalka.com/moms and purchase the Walka Mom program (the “Program”). This page is a web storefront, not the Walka app itself; the data practices here (ad tracking pixels, checkout, email reminders) are different from the main Walka app's, which is why this is a separate policy. By using this page, you agree to this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 You Provide

  • First name and email, entered at checkout.
  • Payment details — handled directly by Stripe; we never receive or store your card number.

2.2 Automatically Collected

  • Approximate country, detected from your IP address at the edge (via Vercel), used only to determine eligibility and whether to charge in USD or CAD. We do not store your IP address or a more precise location.
  • Browsing and checkout behavior — pages viewed, which checkout step you reached, session duration — via PostHog, for product analytics and to know when to send a reminder email if you don't finish checking out.
  • Advertising events — via the Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok Pixel. These automatically log that a page was viewed, and we additionally send a one-time “Purchase” event containing only the price and currency actually charged. We never send your name, email, health information, or anything about the Program's content to Meta or TikTok.
  • Standard device/browser info (browser type, OS, screen size) collected by the above analytics tools.

2.3 We Do Not Collect Here

This page never collects health data, step counts, or HealthKit/Google Fit data of any kind — that only happens later, inside the Walka app itself, after you've downloaded it and created an account, governed by the main Walka Privacy Policy.

2.4 Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your progress through checkout, so you can leave and come back without starting over. PostHog, the Meta Pixel, and the TikTok Pixel each set their own cookies/identifiers for analytics and ad measurement; see their respective policies for how to opt out of ad tracking generally (e.g., your device's Limit Ad Tracking / App Tracking Transparency settings, or Meta's and TikTok's ad preference tools).

3. How We Use Information

We use your information to: process your payment; email you a receipt and, if you don't finish checkout, up to two reminder emails (sent via Resend) before giving up; measure which ads and pages lead to a purchase; and detect and prevent fraud.

5. Data Retention

Checkout records (including abandoned, never-completed checkouts) are retained to support refund requests and for fraud prevention, and are deleted or anonymized on request (Section 6) subject to the payment-record retention below. Payment records are retained per legal and tax requirements (typically 7 years).

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your personal information, and to opt out of any future sale or sharing of it (we do not sell or share it). To exercise these rights, email susan@getwalka.com.

California (CCPA/CPRA): California residents have the rights described above and the right to non-discrimination for exercising them. We do not sell or share personal information as defined under CCPA/CPRA.

UK and EEA (GDPR): Our legal bases for processing are performance of contract (to deliver the Program you purchased), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, analytics), and consent (ad tracking cookies, where applicable). You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

This page is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact susan@getwalka.com and we will delete it.

8. International Users

getwalka.com/moms is currently offered to residents of the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and Nigeria. If you are located outside these countries, please do not use this page. If you do, you acknowledge that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

9. Third-Party Services

Stripe's, PostHog's, Meta's, TikTok's, and Resend's own privacy policies also apply to your use of their services. Walka has no control over how these providers handle your data within their own systems beyond what's described above.
